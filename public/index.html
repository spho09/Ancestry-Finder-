Sample Details

Why SNP frequencies differ by population: the markers used here are ancestry-informative SNPs — positions in the genome where the frequency of each variant differs across populations because of demographic history: geographic isolation, migration, and genetic drift accumulated over tens of thousands of years. No single SNP determines ancestry; the pattern across thousands of them, compared statistically to reference populations, is what produces a similarity estimate.
Training data: 1000 Genomes Phase 3 reference panel (2,504 individuals, 26 populations). Test data: your uploaded genome, never used to build the reference model. Results describe genetic similarity to these reference populations, not self-identified ethnicity or exact genealogical ancestry.
