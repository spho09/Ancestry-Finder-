Leave-one-out cross-validation

Model validation

Every one of the 2,504 individuals in the 1000 Genomes reference panel was scored by temporarily removing their own contribution to their population's PCA centroid, spread, and allele frequencies, then running them through the exact same scoring code the live app uses for a real upload. This page is generated from that run, not from hand-picked examples.
